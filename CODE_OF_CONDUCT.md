# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal images or health information.
- Accept correction gracefully and focus feedback on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and other
abusive conduct are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the security
contact in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and applies to all project
spaces.
